# Template assets and licensing

- Use Clippster-created/commissioned assets or independently licensed assets that explicitly permit redistribution as editable template source material.
- A rendered-video license is insufficient. Preserve proof for editable redistribution, modification, embedding, commercial use, platforms, territories, and expiry.
- Track composition and master-recording music rights separately, including synchronization rights.
- Bundle font license text and verify embedding, redistribution, and modification rights.
- Do not copy TikTok/CapCut templates, effects, music, branding, APIs, or proprietary formats. Their platform materials are not assumed portable.
- Record AI-generated/modified disclosures and performer/model/trademark permissions where applicable.
- Revocation blocks new use but must not damage existing independent instances.

Each asset record requires SHA-256, detected MIME, size, safe relative path, rights holder, license/SPDX or `LicenseRef`, proof reference, and restrictions. Built-in generated card art is documented as original Clippster work; user media is never packaged or redistributed.

FFmpeg build flags/notices and Remotion commercial terms require release review. Legal counsel must approve launch-jurisdiction and asset contracts.
